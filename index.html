Privacy Policy – Shopify Detector

Effective date: September 27, 2025

1. Data Collection

Shopify Detector does not collect, store, or transmit any personal information or browsing data. The extension only checks the current website for publicly available Shopify indicators, such as meta tags, global variables, or asset URLs.

2. Usage of Data

No data leaves your browser. The extension does not track your activity, store history, or share any information with third parties.

3. Third-Party Services

Shopify Detector does not integrate with any third-party services or analytics tools.
